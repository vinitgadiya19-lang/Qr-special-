🛡️ Essential QR & UPI Scam Prevention Guide

🛑

The "Scan to Receive Money" Fraud

Scammers on OLX, WhatsApp, or Telegram tell you they want to buy your item or give you a scholarship by sending you a QR code to "receive payment". Scanning a QR code ALWAYS sends money out of your account; it never receives money.

🔐

The Fake Bank KYC Trap

Phishing QR codes lead to replica login pages asking for your NetBanking credentials, Debit Card details, or OTP under the threat that "Your SIM or Account will be blocked within 24 hours". Banks never ask for KYC updates via QR codes.

🎁

The "Cashback & Lottery" Lure

Fake scratch cards and QR posters in public places promise ₹2,000 lottery cashbacks. When scanned, they open an authorized UPI collect request disguised as a reward redemption.
